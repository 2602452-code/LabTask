Islamabad,Beautiful city

Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan, nestled against the scenic backdrop of the Margalla Hills on the Pothohar Plateau.
Built as a planned city in the 1960s to replace Karachi as the national capital, it was officially inaugurated in 1967 and is organized into distinct, numbered sectors. Renowned for its abundant green spaces, clean layout, and peaceful atmosphere, the city is home to iconic landmarks such as the Bedouin-tent-inspired Faisal Mosque and the star-and-crescent-shaped Pakistan Monument.
